  • Aggregate reports (RUA) arrive roughly daily as XML, summarizing pass/fail counts grouped by sending IP -- exactly the format generated here, per RFC 7489 Appendix C. Domain owners use these to spot legitimate senders they forgot to authorize before moving to p=reject.
  • Forensic reports (RUF) are sent per-failure, with more detail about the specific message (subject to privacy redaction in the real world -- this simulation shows full detail since there's no real user data involved).
