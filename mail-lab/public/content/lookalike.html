Common tricks

  • Typosquatting: mycompanny.local, missing/extra/swapped letters.
  • Homoglyphs: characters that render almost identically -- rn instead of m, vv instead of w, 0 instead of o, or entirely different Unicode characters from other alphabets (Cyrillic а looks identical to Latin a in most fonts).
  • Subdomain tricks: mycompany.local.attacker-domain.com -- the real domain is attacker-domain.com; everything before it is just a subdomain label the attacker controls.

This lab flags candidates using edit-distance (Levenshtein) on both the raw domain and a version with common confusable sequences normalized -- the same basic technique real anti-phishing engines use, extended in production with much larger confusable-character tables and visual similarity models.
