Email authentication is built entirely on DNS TXT records. Before you can understand SPF, DKIM, or DMARC, you need a place to publish and read those records — that's what this page simulates.

In real life you'd log into your domain registrar or DNS host and add TXT records under names like _dmarc.yourdomain.com or selector1._domainkey.yourdomain.com. Here, you create a "domain" (it can be anything, e.g. mycompany.local) and add records to a zone that only this app can see. Every other lab on this site — SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MTA-STS, BIMI — reads from this same simulated zone, exactly like a real mail server would query real DNS.
