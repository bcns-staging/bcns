TLS-RPT (RFC 8460) is the feedback loop for MTA-STS and DANE: it lets a domain owner ask senders to email back a daily JSON summary of how many delivery attempts succeeded or failed TLS validation, and why.

📤
Delivery Attempts
Many senders try delivering to your domain over time
→
📊
Successes + Failures Tallied
Each attempt logged as success or failure, with a reason
→
📧
Daily JSON Report
Sent to the rua= address in your TLS-RPT record

Without TLS-RPT, a domain that misconfigures its MTA-STS or DANE setup (e.g. an expired certificate, or an MX host that no longer matches the policy) would have no visibility that mail is silently failing to deliver -- senders don't bounce these failures back to the sender in any obvious way. Publishing rua=mailto:... in a _smtp._tls.yourdomain.com TXT record gets you those reports.
