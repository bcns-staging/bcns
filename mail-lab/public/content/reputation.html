RBLs / DNSBLs (Realtime Blackhole Lists / DNS-based Blackhole Lists) are shared databases of IP addresses (and sometimes domains) known to send spam or malware, queried in real time during the SMTP conversation. The clever part is the query mechanism: rather than a special protocol, DNSBLs reuse plain DNS. To check IP 1.2.3.4 against a list zen.spamhaus.org, a server queries the reversed octets: 4.3.2.1.zen.spamhaus.org. An A-record response means "listed"; NXDOMAIN means "clean."
