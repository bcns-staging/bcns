MTA-STS (RFC 8461) fixes STARTTLS's biggest weakness: it makes TLS mandatory rather than optional, and pins which mail servers are allowed to receive your domain's mail.

How discovery works

  1. A sender looks up _mta-sts.yourdomain.com TXT. Its only job is to say "a policy exists, with this version id" -- it's not the policy itself.
  2. The sender then fetches the actual policy over HTTPS from https://mta-sts.yourdomain.com/.well-known/mta-sts.txt (we simulate that file locally here).
  3. The policy lists mode (enforce, testing, or none), the authorized mx hosts, and how long to cache it (max_age).

In enforce mode, if the connecting MX doesn't match the policy or TLS isn't available, the sender must refuse to deliver rather than fall back to an unprotected connection. testing mode logs what would have happened (via TLS-RPT) without actually blocking anything -- useful for rolling out a policy safely.
