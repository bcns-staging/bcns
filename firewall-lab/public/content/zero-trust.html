Zero Trust flips the default assumption of network security: instead of "anything inside the network perimeter is trusted," nothing is trusted by default, regardless of which zone it's in -- every connection must be explicitly justified by policy, using the most specific identity available: application, user, and content, not just IP and port.

This is the capstone of everything in this lab's NGFW half: a deny-by-default rulebase built from App-ID, User-ID, and Content-ID together is what makes real micro-segmentation practical -- "the finance app, for the finance group, with content inspected" is a policy statement zone-and-IP rules alone can't express.
