Malware overwhelmingly relies on DNS to find its command-and-control infrastructure. DNS security intercepts resolution for known-bad domains and substitutes a sinkhole address instead of the real one -- the infected host's lookups still "succeed," but it ends up talking to a controlled, harmless destination instead of the real attacker, and the attempted resolution itself becomes a loud, unambiguous signal that something on that host needs investigating.
