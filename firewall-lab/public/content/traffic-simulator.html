This is the flagship lab: build one connection and run it through either rulebase, or both at once. In Side-by-side mode, the exact same connection is evaluated by the Traditional and NGFW engines independently, and any place they disagree is called out explicitly -- this is the clearest possible demonstration of what an NGFW actually buys you over a traditional firewall.

The three preset scenarios are the canonical stories: an application disguising itself on port 443, malware riding inside a session that looks completely ordinary at the IP/port level, and a URL-category violation on a destination that's technically allowed. In every case, Traditional allows the traffic (it looks like nothing more than "tcp/443 to an allowed zone") while NGFW catches the real story underneath.
