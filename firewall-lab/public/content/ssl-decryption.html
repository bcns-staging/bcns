The majority of traffic today is encrypted, which means every content-based inspection engine -- URL filtering, IPS, anti-malware, threat intel -- is completely blind to it by default. SSL/TLS decryption (a "forward proxy" decrypt) solves this: the firewall terminates the TLS connection, inspects the plaintext with its normal content engines, then re-encrypts it on the way out. Without a decrypt profile attached to the matching rule, encrypted sessions simply skip every content-ID stage -- allowed or blocked on zone/IP/app alone, with whatever is actually inside completely unexamined.
