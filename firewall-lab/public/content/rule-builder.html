Detectors and lookups on their own don't decide anything -- the rule base is what turns them into a decision. This page is where every technique from the pages above gets combined: pick a ruleset, arrange rules in the order they should be evaluated, and test traffic against them live.

The Traditional ruleset only matches on zones, IPs, protocol, and port -- exactly the 5-tuple. The NGFW ruleset accepts everything Traditional does, plus application, user/group, and URL category, and lets you attach an inspection profile (IPS, anti-malware, URL filtering, threat intel, SSL decrypt) to each rule. Match fields left as any match everything for that field.
