Threat intelligence feeds are curated lists of IPs and domains known to be malicious -- command-and-control servers, phishing infrastructure, scanning hosts -- contributed by security vendors and researchers. Checking every connection's destination against a feed catches threats no signature or content inspection would recognize, because the indicator itself is what's bad, independent of what the traffic actually contains.
