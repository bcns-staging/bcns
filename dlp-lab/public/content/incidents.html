Detection plus policy gets you a decision; the incident console is where a human closes the loop. Every policy match that isn't a clean "allow" -- a block, a quarantine, a redaction, an encryption, or even a justification prompt -- lands here as a case to review.

This is also your cross-channel view: filter by user to see everything one person has triggered across email, cloud uploads, endpoint actions, and file-share scans in one place -- exactly the correlation a real security team relies on to notice a pattern that no single channel would reveal on its own (e.g., the same user hitting a block on email right after a near-miss on a cloud upload).

Every case gets a status: open (needs review), in review, resolved (confirmed and handled), or false positive (the policy was wrong to flag it -- useful signal for tuning thresholds later).
