Building a DLP policy for a specific regulation from a blank page is slow and easy to get wrong. In practice, organizations start from a vendor- or industry-provided rule pack -- a pre-built bundle of keyword lists, detectors, and thresholds tuned for a specific framework -- and adjust from there rather than starting from zero.

This lab includes four starter packs covering the regulations that drive the large majority of real-world DLP requirements:

  • GDPR -- EU personal data, identity documents, data-subject-rights language.
  • HIPAA -- US protected health information (PHI).
  • PCI-DSS -- payment card data, leaning heavily on the checksum-validated card detector.
  • CCPA -- California consumer privacy, structurally similar to GDPR.
