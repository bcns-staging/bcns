Data in use is the hardest DLP surface to cover, because none of it ever touches the network: copying sensitive text to the clipboard, pasting it into a chat app, saving it to a USB drive, or printing it. Email and web gateways are completely blind to all of this -- it requires an agent running directly on the endpoint.

Removable media control (blocking or restricting USB devices) is modeled here as just another endpoint action type -- from a policy's perspective it's the same evaluation, just with a different destination label.
